What Makes It "Not Quite RPG"?

No open-world grind loop. The game is the descent: gear up on the surface, go down with your party, take what the lair guards and come back faster next time.

A Lair for Every Party

Each party gets its own fresh copy of the lair. Minibosses must fall in order, and each kill breaks a seal of old stonework so the way deeper opens. When the boss dies a portal appears; touch it and you return to the spot you descended from.

Skills Are the Weapon

Plain sword swings do nothing. Every weapon hit is a skill cast from the item itself, and it rolls against the target's defense, dodge and accuracy, with critical hits on top. Bosses and their minions carry the same stats, scaled by level.

Gear That Grows With You

Rarities, item sets with tiered bonuses, and typed gem sockets. Ability gems grant a skill, one of each per item; attribute gems stack. Gems can be removed and kept, and every stat line shows what is the item's own and what your gems add.

Clear Times & World Firsts

Every counted run lands on the boards: best times, clears and fastest parties, solo and party kept apart, all-time and weekly. The first group to kill a listed boss is recorded for good and announced to the whole server.

Parties

Descend together and share experience from kills. Friendly fire is real: careless damage skills hit your own party.

Guilds

Tags, three ranks, up to 30 members and a moderated guild chat. Guildmates are sworn: they can never harm each other, but buffs still land.

Marks of Renown

Cosmetic auras show who came back from below, alongside a cinematic main menu where you choose your starting weapon.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I play right now?

Not yet. Vaelithra is in private development and testing. The public beta date will be announced on Discord.

Is Vaelithra a Java or Bedrock server?

Java Edition only, running on Paper for Minecraft 1.21.11.

Do I need client mods?

No. A vanilla client is enough; the server sends a resource pack for custom icons, item models and interface glyphs.

Can I hurt other players?

Vaelithra is PvE first. Inside a party, friendly fire is on, so damage and debuff skills can hit your own party. Members of the same guild can never harm each other, even in a party, while buffs still reach them.

How do the leaderboards work?

Each lair keeps best times, clears and failed runs, plus a fastest-parties board. Solo runs and party runs (two or more players) are ranked separately, both all-time and weekly. Staff test runs never count. World firsts record the first group to kill each listed boss, once and forever. The same boards stand in-game with a podium of the top five players.
