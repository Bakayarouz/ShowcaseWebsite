Frequently Asked Questions

Is Vaelithra a Java or Bedrock Minecraft server?

Vaelithra is built specifically for **Minecraft Java Edition** (1.20.4+). You can join using a standard vanilla Java client; our custom UI and boss models load dynamically via server-side resourcepack prompts.

How does the PvE focus work with PvP?

PvE is our primary focus. All core progression, dungeons, gear upgrades, and bosses are entirely PvE. PvP acts as a voluntary side dish available in opt-in battle arenas, special contested Lairs, and scheduled Guild Wars.

Do I need heavy client mods to play?

No client mods or Forge/Fabric setups are strictly required! We leverage server-side Paper pipelines, custom packets, and ModelEngine to deliver animated bosses and custom HUD elements seamlessly.

How do Guild Wars and Leaderboards function?

Guilds can capture outer regional outposts to gain active bonus yields. Speedrun leaderboards automatically track instanced mythic dungeon clear times and register world-first boss slays.
